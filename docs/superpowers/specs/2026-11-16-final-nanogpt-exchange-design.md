# Final NanoGPT comparison of conservative velocity exchange

This is the final neural comparison authorized for the course report. The synthetic pair-collision experiments motivated transferring velocity between replicas. The earlier NanoGPT impulse experiment had zero activated impulses, and the later single-replica damping experiment tested a different control. The primary question is whether an activated exchange lowers the selected final test cross-entropy relative to independent Momentum SGD at the same gradient budget. AdamW is the practical reference.

- Use the existing 108,352-parameter character-level NanoGPT, Tiny Shakespeare, chronological 80/10/10 train/validation/test splits, CPU float32, one Torch thread, batch eight, context 64, dropout zero, and gradient norm clipping at one. Use the previous learning rates and moment coefficients: Momentum SGD 0.3 and 0.9, AdamW 0.001 and (0.9, 0.95), zero weight decay. No hyperparameter search or early stopping.
- For each seed group use four independent initialization and minibatch streams with seeds `4*s+i`, `i=0,1,2,3`. Pair the streams and initial states across the two Momentum populations. AdamW uses stream and initial state zero through 400 updates. Shuffle method order deterministically per seed. Engineering seed 5100. Formal seeds 5110–5119. The formal seeds are new, while the corpus and test partition were used in earlier experiments.
- Both populations perform four runs of 100 updates. Exchange pairs after local updates 20, 40, 60, and 80, cycling through `(0,1)/(2,3)`, `(0,2)/(1,3)`, and `(0,3)/(1,2)`. Exchange the complete native SGD momentum buffers. Their signed displacements are `-lr * buffer`. The trigger uses the update count. The exchange leaves parameters at their current points and takes no extra gradient or loss queries. The independent arm skips exchange. AdamW runs 400 updates. Each arm receives 400 minibatch gradient evaluations, or 204,800 training token positions.
- Score every final candidate on 32 common fixed validation minibatches (evaluation seed 71001). Select by lowest final validation cross-entropy, breaking ties by index. Score the selected final state on 32 common fixed test minibatches. Record validation and test forwards separately from gradients. Stop at the exact budget or record a failure. Save initial and endpoint hashes, per-update training losses and gradient norms, endpoint checkpoints, actual counts, selected candidate, event times, buffer hashes and energy transfer. Audit the common trajectory before the first exchange and exact buffer permutation. Compute momentum component residual in infinity norm and energy residual in absolute value using float64. Confirm identical position hashes across each event, nonidentical swapped velocities, and both residuals at most 1e-12. The exchange supplies and dissipates zero energy. Gradient forcing and native momentum damping occur at ordinary updates.
- Report mean and sample SD of final test losses and paired differences (exchange minus independent, exchange minus AdamW). Resample whole seed groups for a descriptive percentile 95% paired bootstrap interval (20,000 replicates, RNG seed 5239). The engineering success criterion is eight nontrivial swaps per seed and audited budgets and invariants. Evidence of an exchange benefit requires a negative primary difference with an interval below zero. Include all formal seed groups and disclose failures. The experiment tests scheduled equal-mass swaps under one fixed setting. No adaptive timing, contact geometry, or curvature gate is applied to NanoGPT.
